      SCENARIO RESULT

      —expected accepted jobs

      See the calculation and limits

      Admitted jobs = minimum of offers, worker capacity, review capacity and fully reserved budget. Expected accepted jobs = admitted × the assumed acceptance rate, rounded down. Every attempt incurs execution and review costs. Only accepted work earns the modeled reward; unused reward reserves are released. All amounts are whole USDC planning units.

      Acceptance rates are assumptions. This scenario does not estimate realized buyer value, revenue, profit, disputes, refund timing, gas, taxes, fees or settlement finality. A $40 trillion/year screen-work opportunity is a user-supplied strategic scenario, not a verified market size or a revenue forecast.

      03 / A TRACEABLE PATH TO VALUE

      Every outcome earns its next step.

      A good result can be inspected,
      reproduced and put to use.

      01

      Scope & authorize

      Specify inputs, applications, budgets, deliverables and acceptance criteria. Check worker and reviewer capacity.

      02

      Execute & capture

      A configured worker uses approved tools, APIs or computer use. Record versions, actions, costs, failures and artifact hashes.

      03

      Review & accept

      A separate reviewer verifies the actual artifacts. The buyer tests usefulness. Rejected work returns for repair or resolution.

      04

      Settle & learn

      Apply the deployed settlement rules after authorization. Promote improvements only after measured evaluation and a rollback check.
