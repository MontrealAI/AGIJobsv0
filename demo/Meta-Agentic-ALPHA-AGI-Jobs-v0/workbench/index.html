      ILLUSTRATIVE ANNUAL ACCEPTED VOLUME
      
            —
            
      
            
            
      
            
      
              Assumptions: 365 operating days, $1 per USDC, sufficient demand up
              to a chosen $40T/year market ceiling. This is a scenario, not
              measured TAM, revenue, liquidity, a price forecast, or a
              deployment benchmark. Costs, disputes, recovery and customer
              acquisition are not modeled.

          From useful digital work
      to extraordinary physical possibility.
