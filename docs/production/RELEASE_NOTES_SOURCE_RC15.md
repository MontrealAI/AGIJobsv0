mathematics research question, the current approach, the best credible
alternative, a hard outcome and its constraints, public source URLs and the
evidence text supplied with them. The record distinguishes user-supplied
evidence from an independently checked source. A URL or pasted excerpt alone

strategy candidates, comparisons, rejected attempts, proposed changes and

## Reproduce a bounded strategy comparison

The [supplier Computer Use laboratory](https://montrealai.github.io/AGIJobsv0/successor/computer/)
is a secondary validation example. Its disclosed arithmetic and real browser
protocol exercise make the distinction between a proposed strategy, an observed
interaction and independent proof inspectable before using the separate Studio
handoff for a real mission.

The disclosed fictional buyer needs 40 units: at least 10 by day 7 and all 40 by
day 14, within USD 510.00. Buying all 40 from Stellar costs USD 490.00. A staged
recommendation buys 10 from Stellar and 30 from Laurentian for USD 360.00,
including both suppliers' shipping charges. The USD 130.00 difference is exact
arithmetic on synthetic offers, not observed customer savings or a claim about
real inventory, delivery or procurement.

**Level 3 develops the strategy itself.** The engine constructs typed programs
from a finite grammar with 24 combinations of allocation, ordering and cost
operators, with up to two failure-driven revisions. Each attempt retains its
program, execution, logical-operation use, result and lineage. A candidate is
frozen before two distinct generated public fresh cases are evaluated. A
changed candidate needs a new record. These inspectable cases are not protected
independent holdouts, and the bounded grammar does not establish open-ended
strategy invention.

**Level 4 examines an extra assumption.** A local, explicit comparison can relax
the extra assumption that all units must come from one supplier or arrive early.
The buyer's actual quantities, deadlines, budget and recommendation-only scope
remain fixed and checked. If all 40 units really are required by day 7, a late
shipment remains invalid and the USD 490.00 feasible incumbent remains the
comparison. This is bounded outcome-preserving reformulation, not a claim of
general transformational intelligence.

A credible conventional two-delivery comparison also finds USD 360.00. The
constructed strategy therefore establishes **no same-task superiority** over
that alternative and no independently proven AI Alpha. The USD 490.00-to-360.00
comparison changes the additional workflow assumption. The institutional
decision remains `INCUMBENT_RETAINED_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`; a useful
nominee does not authorize replacement.

The public supplier desk accepts keyboard and mouse interaction, recomputes
quantities, shipping, deadlines and budget, and displays the exact observed
allocation result. Keep the readable brief, program lineage, frozen comparison,
fresh-case results and mission together. Reopening a report replays its
bindings. A checksum does not authenticate an author, provider or reviewer;
imported execution claims cannot grant authority or become independent proof.
