## Arithmetic and units

All payout producers pass actual AGIALPHA base units (18 decimals), without the inherited extra factor of 10^12. For current score C below M=88,888 and nonnegative nominal points P:

`gain = min(M-C, floor(P*M*M/(M*M+C*C)))`

Full-precision multiplication/division prevents intermediate overflow. At or above M, growth is a no-op; zero points preserve the score. Existing score is never damped or subtracted by a positive award. Integer rounding can yield zero, including tiny credits at positive scores. Validator rewards use the actual credited agent gain, not the nominal award. Claims apply their own current-score damping when processed, so transaction order can affect rounding and credited amounts.

The existing payout curve is retained after correcting units: whole-token payout is cubed, integer-divided by 100,000, multiplied by 1,000,000 and passed through the existing integer log2 routine. This intentionally retains a cliff: payouts of 46 whole tokens yield 0 points; 47 yield 20; 100 yield 24; 1,000 yield 34; 1,000,000 yield 64 before score damping. Fractional tokens are floored. There is no hidden minimum award. Inputs above twice uint128 maximum are rejected, matching the current reward width and maximum 200% payout bound.

Elapsed duration earns no points in v3. Submission time is recorded once as audit data, preventing buyer/arbitration delay or deliberate waiting from increasing reputation. The legacy duration parameter/constant remains for ABI compatibility only.

## Agent policy and exact-once accounting

Creation snapshots failure rate, cap and validator-reward percentage. Assignment snapshots the agent score and `min(cap, ceil(score*rate/10000))`. Candidate defaults are 1000bps and 10 points, an engineering choice without a claim of economic optimality. The owner can set 0–10000bps and a 0–88,888-point cap. At terminal attributable failure the debit is capped again by current score. Concurrent jobs therefore cannot underflow a balance. No new failure debit sets the blacklist flag.

Default admission threshold remains zero. An owner-configured positive threshold can be crossed by a penalty, preventing new assignments. Existing assigned jobs and dispute/settlement paths remain usable; a later earning success can restore score. Small zero-point jobs cannot restore eligibility. Authorized governance restoration is the remaining documented recovery path; there is no automatic recovery or hidden floor immunity.

Keys include registry address and job ID, with agent and policy binding. Provisional validation does not credit or debit the agent. Final success is credited once; final explicit attributed breach is debited once. Cancellation, unassigned expiry and unattributed employer refund have no new reputation debit. Legacy employer-win financial refund/slash semantics remain unchanged. A financial refund must not be described as proof of agent wrongdoing.

Versioned attribution values are 1=successful outcome, 2=unattributed employer refund, 3=attributed agent breach. A nonzero evidence hash binds the authorized adjudicator's record, not its truth or independence. Every later resolution replaces prior attribution. Boolean resolutions remain unattributed. Signed Boolean and attributed resolutions bind module, chain, registry, job and current dispute round; old v2 Boolean payloads are invalid and signer clients must migrate.

Immutable packed per-index facts record the actual selected committee and reveal/vote states at closure. Later owner size changes do not truncate records. Individual permissionless claims use an exact-once bitmap and constant per-claim work. There is no claim expiry or pruning. Round recording remains linear; accounting-only measurements were about 2.47M gas at 100 validators and 6.19M at 256. These numbers are not full validation-closure gas limits. Default and larger complete-round benchmarks, operational maximums and final compiler evidence remain qualification gates; no hidden new validator cap is imposed.

Honest disagreement and appeal reversals are possible. Protocol-outcome scoring must not be advertised as detection of dishonesty. Ten-percent collateral alone does not establish incentive compatibility: validation quality, detection probability, collusion, access value, reward cliffs, claim ordering and governance trust matter. Discounted finite scenarios are regression examples, not an equilibrium proof.

Registry authorization and Engine pause state are settlement dependencies. Do not revoke a registry's Engine authorization, retire an Engine, or leave it paused while it has unsettled jobs or unclaimed validator accounting. New jobs can select a replacement compatible Engine, while existing jobs retain their recorded Engine. Governance must reconcile both engines' outstanding records and recovery controls before retiring either stack. No automatic migration of old v2 obligations, earned scores or claims is performed.

## Local validator gas envelope

The preserved [closure benchmark report](evidence/terminal-v3-closure-gas/REPORT.md) and hash-bound bundle measure the preceding runtime freeze, before the combined candidate’s warning-only legacy-marker mutability cleanup. They are diagnostic local results; exact integrated canonical and target-network qualification remain pending.

Three selected validators passed selection and closure in the tested Osaka profile, including dense pools of 3, 100 and 256 and a sparse 256-member pool with only three eligible members. Actual 100- and 256-member selection exceeded the 16,777,216 transaction gas cap and is unsupported in that tested profile. A separate Cancun diagnostic measured 100-member closure; 256-member closure remains unmeasured after resource-limited attempts. No maximum-validator setting was silently reduced. The configured maximum is not the default selected committee size.

Fixtures and zero-fee/reward assumptions are explicitly listed in the report; they do not qualify the complete production configuration. Receipt gas after refunds is not the required execution gas. The existing sampling limit bounds accepted candidates rather than visited pool entries, so an untested larger or sparse pool can still exceed available gas. Activation must use a separately qualified operational envelope.
