# AGI Jobs Participant Tax Responsibilities

Version 1.0. Published October 17, 2026.

This version applies only when selected and presented for participant acknowledgement, subject to applicable notice and consent requirements. Publication does not change an existing contract or acknowledgement retroactively.

## Scope

This policy describes tax responsibilities associated with participating in AGI Jobs as an employer, agent, validator, or other participant. “You” means the person or entity participating, including through a wallet or automated agent. “Platform Parties” means the persons and entities providing or maintaining the AGI Jobs software or interfaces, in those capacities. This description does not determine any person's legal status, employment relationship, or obligations under applicable law.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for identifying and complying with the tax obligations that applicable law imposes on you in connection with your participation. These may include income or corporate tax, capital gains tax, sales taxes including GST/HST, QST or VAT, payroll obligations, withholding, registration, information reporting, and recordkeeping.

You must determine the appropriate treatment of your activities, calculate and pay amounts due, make required filings and remittances on time, and obtain qualified independent advice when necessary. Using an automated agent, smart contract, token, or wallet does not remove these responsibilities. You must not use the platform to conceal taxable activity or evade legal obligations.

Employers must assess their obligations in funding and paying for work, including any payroll, withholding, sales-tax or reporting duties. Agents and validators must assess their obligations for compensation and rewards, and for subsequent transactions involving those assets. Other participants must assess their own activities and holdings under applicable law. Role names used by the software do not decide tax or employment classification.

## Transactions and records

Token receipts, payments, exchanges, transfers, rewards, fees, burns and other events may have tax consequences. Their treatment depends on the facts and applicable law. This policy does not promise capital rather than business treatment, a deduction, recognition of a loss, a particular valuation, or the absence of a filing obligation. A token burn is not represented as a charitable donation or an automatically deductible expense.

Keep complete, accurate records for the period required by applicable law. As relevant, retain transaction dates and identifiers, wallet addresses, asset quantities, acquisition costs, fair market values and valuation methods, currency conversions, fees, invoices, payment purposes, and supporting records for rewards, transfers and burns. Keep the policy version and acknowledgement relevant to your participation. Blockchain records and explorer displays may not contain everything needed for a return. Do not rely on continued access to an interface or third-party service as your only recordkeeping method.

## No tax advice or assurance

The software, interfaces, documentation, estimates, labels and acknowledgement records are provided for operational information. They do not constitute personalized tax, legal or accounting advice, an opinion from a tax authority, or a representation that a transaction or person is exempt. The Platform Parties do not act as your tax adviser or representative merely by making the platform available. Obtain advice appropriate to your jurisdiction and circumstances before relying on a tax treatment.

No particular tax outcome, deduction, exemption, reporting treatment, or acceptance by an authority is promised. Any voluntary tax-related service would require a separate express agreement. This does not limit duties imposed directly by applicable law.

## Allocation subject to law

As between you and the Platform Parties, you bear the taxes and compliance costs that applicable law imposes on you, including interest or penalties attributable to your failure to meet your own obligations. Your participation does not create a promise by the Platform Parties to reimburse those amounts or prepare your returns.

Each Platform Party remains responsible for any obligation that applicable law imposes on it. This policy does not transfer, eliminate or excuse mandatory collection, withholding, remittance, reporting, recordkeeping or other duties, and does not bind a tax authority. No exemption is claimed merely because software is decentralized, a service is free, tokens are burned, or a person does not receive a particular payment.

Nothing in this policy excludes liability or restricts rights where doing so is prohibited by law, including applicable consumer protections. No indemnity, waiver of claims, or monetary liability cap is created by this policy.

## Software flags and acknowledgement

A function, field, comment or interface label such as `isTaxExempt` is a software implementation detail. A value of `true` is not a tax ruling, exemption certificate, or legal conclusion about the protocol, its participants, or the Platform Parties. Descriptions of software behavior must be checked against the relevant deployed version and configuration.

An on-chain acknowledgement records an address and policy version according to the software's rules. It does not itself establish a person's identity, authority, comprehension, legal capacity, or valid consent under every applicable law. When acting for another person or entity, you must have authority to do so.
