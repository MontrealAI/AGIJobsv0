The public Onebox teaching workflow is a simulation. Its create/assign/submit/validate/finalize confirmations and exported events retain `simulated=true`, `productionApproved=false` and `chainTransactions=0`. The automatic validator's HTTP(S) result reader now shares the existing operator-origin policy: redirects are rejected, transfers abort after 15 seconds, and response streams stop above 4 MiB. Setting `ORCHESTRATOR_ARTIFACT_ORIGINS` replaces the default origin list and grants every path at each admitted origin. A separately configured gateway adds only its checked route when its origin is not already admitted. Hostname approval is operator trust, not DNS pinning or proof of external content; scoped deployment and egress controls remain necessary. Local-result preference and inline data-URI handling retain their existing behavior.
