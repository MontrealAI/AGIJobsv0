# Find your Alpha and build your SUCCESSOR Ω

Start with a useful result that you can inspect and keep. The flagship connects
method development, evidence and bounded work while preserving the current approach
when improvement has not been established.

## Try the free flagship

Open [Find your Alpha](https://montrealai.github.io/AGIJobsv0/successor/alpha/)
([Français](https://montrealai.github.io/AGIJobsv0/successor/alpha/?lang=fr)).
Reconcile two public or synthetic CSV catalogs, preserve duplicate rows, inspect
unmatched entries and discrepancies, then download the output, report and reusable
capability. The working browser workspace can be kept offline.

Optional strategy development composes bounded programs, records attempts, freezes
one candidate and compares it on a new public synthetic case against declared
baselines. The local objective counts logical work and an auxiliary-row resource
proxy. It does not measure complete memory, independent superiority or economic
advantage. A losing candidate stays on HOLD and the incumbent is retained.

The native handoff compiles actual AGI Jobs work orders for the same bounded catalog
mission. They are unsigned, unfunded drafts. The installed native runner and browser
SDK rehearsal are separate paths with separately checked return files.

## Build an evidence brief from a public source

Open the [source mission](https://montrealai.github.io/AGIJobsv0/successor/research/)
([Français](https://montrealai.github.io/AGIJobsv0/successor/research/fr/)).
Use the clearly labeled original example or paste public, non-personal text that
you have permission to use. Set the question and excerpt budget. Compare workflows
under that same budget, inspect exact passages and offsets, and save a readable
brief and complete dossier.

The workflows combine goal matching and coverage heuristics, including a reframing
of the output as a reviewable evidence map. Random and sham controls expose the
limits of mechanical scoring. These workflows extract text; they do not establish
semantic truth, understand a book, evaluate battery safety or certify an advantage.
Source URLs identify user-supplied attribution; the browser does not fetch or
authenticate them. Human review of relevance and consequential omissions remains
necessary.

Freeze a method before using a different source for a subsequent check. Retain the
current method until the agreed independent evidence supports replacement. Preserve
failed attempts and the cost of review as well as promising results. Export before
closing; this page does not upload or automatically save the source.

## What the stages mean

| Stage | Useful result | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alpha | A specific advantage worth building, with costs and constraints | A hypothesis is not a demonstrated win |
| Gym | Competing methods and reproducible local observations | Training does not certify capability |
| Specialist ASI | A mission-specific capability that beats Current and the best credible alternative on agreed fresh protected work | The browser example does not award this designation |
| SUCCESSOR Ω | Retained methods, evidence, skills and operating memory after proof and explicit bounded authorization | Local exports grant no execution or financial authority |
